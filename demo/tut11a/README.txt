The 4-bit counter from Magic's tutorial 11 ("Using IRSIM and RSIM with Magic"):
tut11a.mag (the counter), tut11b.mag and tut11c.mag (its bit cells) and tut11d.mag
(the cell both use), and tut11a.cmd (the tutorial's IRSIM command file), copied
unchanged from src/doc/tutcells/ in this repository, under Magic's licence (LICENSE
at the top). counter.cmd, written for the web app, drives the counter through the
tutorial's steps: run "irsim", then "@ counter.cmd".
