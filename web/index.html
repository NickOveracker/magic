Mouse (box tool)

Left
Move the box (its lower left corner) to the pointer
Right
Move the box's upper right corner to the pointer
Middle, Alt+left
Paint the layer under the pointer into the box (or erase on empty space)
Wheel
Scroll; Shift+wheel scrolls sideways; Ctrl+wheel or pinch zooms

Keys (with the pointer in a window)

:
Type a command (also the box below)
v, z, Z
View all, zoom in to the box, zoom out
s, a, i
Select under the box: paint, visible, cell
c, m, d
Copy, move, delete the selection
u, U
Undo, redo
?
Explain the DRC error under the box
Arrows
Scroll; Ctrl+arrows grow the box
